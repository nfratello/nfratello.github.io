Style guide v1.0

One look across the Brief, The Hub and The Coach.

Built from Microsoft brand guidance and the feedback we gathered styling the Brief.

Rules

Twelve rules every page follows.

Canvas

White and off-white sections with small pops of color. Navy only for the top bar, the hero and the footer.

Hero

Photo on the right 62 percent, with the navy fade from the left: solid to 40 percent, soft by 55 percent, clear by 80 percent. People and the device sit in the right of the photo. Keep it: it is our signature.

Colors per page

Neutrals, navy and one industry or site color. No more than three colors working together.

Industry colors

Accents only: the label, a 4 px rule, a tile edge, a chip dot. Never a full background. Always show the industry name next to the color.

Type

Segoe Sans, falling back to Segoe UI. Three sizes per page: headline (34 to 56), section head (26 to 34), body (17). Labels in small capitals above headlines.

Microsoft logo

Four-square in the upper left of the top bar, at least 16 px, with clear space equal to its height. Full color on navy. To confirm with the brand team before going public.

Product icons

Official Dynamics 365 icons from Microsoft Learn, never changed, cropped or recolored, always next to the product name. Used to show how products work together: product labels, the three layer cards and section headers about Dynamics 365. Never as our logo or as decoration.

Copilot and Cowork

Product name as text until we confirm an icon and its terms.

Spacing

4 px grid: 4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 48, 64. Cards 16 px corners and 24 px padding. Sections 56 px top and bottom.

Photos

Real industry scenes, faces visible, screens facing the people using them, natural light, uncluttered.

Product screens

Flat and straight on, one screen at a time, never angled or stacked.

Words

Plain sentences. Product name first, then what it does. No emoji or arrows. Contoso only on public pages.
