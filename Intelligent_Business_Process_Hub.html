Hospitality | Service and operations

Disruption recovery: one storm, one night

Amara Okafor, Duty Manager, Contoso Airport Hotel

A storm strands 212 arriving guests. Recovered in minutes, not hours.

CoworkDynamics 365 Contact CenterDynamics 365 Customer ServiceCopilot Studio
Minutesto a recovery plan, not hours
203 of 212guests served by the agent
9cases that needed a person, with context
Same nightrevenue response
Better together

Dynamics 365: Runs the transaction: guest records, loyalty, cases and every booking change, in Contact Center and Customer Service.

Agents: Does the work around the clock: reaches 212 guests at once, rebooks, applies comp and books the shuttle.

Cowork: Where people direct the work: the briefing, the plan, the approval, the dashboards and the morning review.

Discovery questions
  • What happened the last time weather or an oversell hit one of your properties?
  • How long does it take to know which guests are affected?
  • Who decides comp, and how consistent is it?
  • How many of those guests could have been served without a call?
  • When does revenue management find out?
  • What does leadership see the next morning?
Seller kit: LinkedIn post

A storm closes the airport and 212 arriving guests are stranded. What happens next usually runs on phones and spreadsheets. In this Contoso Hospitality storyboard, Copilot Cowork builds the briefing and the recovery plan, an agent in Dynamics 365 Contact Center reaches every guest, and the duty manager only handles the 9 cases that need a person. Cowork is where people direct the work; Dynamics 365 is where the transaction lives.

Hospitality | Sales

Group and events sales: from RFP to the master bill

Kevin Tran, Group Sales Manager, Contoso Downtown

From RFP to proposal to a clean master bill, without weeks of back and forth.

CoworkDynamics 365 SalesDynamics 365 FinanceCopilot Studio
Same dayRFP response
Minutesto a proposal draft
1 placefor event changes
Before checkoutbill reconciled
Better together

Dynamics 365: Sales holds the account, opportunity, quote and room block. Finance holds the master bill, deposit and collections.

Agents: An intake agent built in Copilot Studio turns every RFP email into a qualified opportunity and a first answer within the hour.

Cowork: Where the team decides: the proposal, the displacement call, the event changes and the bill check before checkout.

Discovery questions
  • How fast do you answer a group RFP today?
  • How many RFPs do you decline for lack of time?
  • How do you decide if a group is worth the displacement?
  • How many master bills end in a dispute?
  • How long from event end to cash collected?
Seller kit: LinkedIn post

Group RFPs often wait days for an answer. In this Contoso Hospitality storyboard, an intake agent turns each RFP email into a qualified opportunity in Dynamics 365 Sales, Copilot Cowork drafts the proposal and the displacement view, and Dynamics 365 Finance closes the bill before checkout. One process, from first email to cash.

Hospitality | Finance and operations

Procure-to-pay and prime cost: from the dock to the GM's Monday

Sofia Bianchi, Director of Food and Beverage, Contoso Downtown

From the receiving dock to a Monday food and labor cost view for the GM.

CoworkDynamics 365 Supply Chain ManagementDynamics 365 FinanceCopilot Studio
136 of 142invoices matched by the agent
Same morningsupplier issues resolved
Every Mondayprime cost by outlet
This weeknot last month
Better together

Dynamics 365: Supply Chain and Finance hold the orders, receipts, vendors, invoices and payments.

Agents: An invoice agent built in Copilot Studio works with vendor invoice automation to match order, receipt and invoice, and drafts the supplier note when something is off.

Cowork: Where leaders see and act: the exception explanation, the Monday prime cost dashboard and the approved actions.

Discovery questions
  • How many days does AP spend each month matching invoices?
  • How often do you pay a price that differs from the order?
  • When does a GM see last week's food and labor cost?
  • Which outlet surprised you most last quarter?
Seller kit: LinkedIn post

Most GMs see last month's food and labor cost, not last week's. In this Contoso Hospitality storyboard, Dynamics 365 Supply Chain Management and Finance run order, receipt, invoice and payment, an invoice agent matches 136 of 142 invoices, and Copilot Cowork gives the GM a Monday prime cost view with actions to approve.
