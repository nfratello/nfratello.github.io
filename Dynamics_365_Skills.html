Get started

How to turn them on

  1. Open Microsoft Copilot, select Cowork, then Customize.
  2. Turn on the Sales plugin (Dynamics 365 Sales) or the Service plugin (Dynamics 365 Customer Service). Without a CRM licence, turn on Sales for Cowork or Service for Cowork for the skills marked "Also works without CRM".
  3. The first time you ask a sales or service question, choose your Dynamics 365 environment. Cowork remembers it.
  4. Just ask in plain words. Cowork picks the right skills, shows its plan, and asks before it changes a record or sends anything.

Your admin controls which plugins are available. Cowork is billed by usage. Details: Use sales skills in Copilot Cowork and Use Customer Service skills in Copilot Cowork.
